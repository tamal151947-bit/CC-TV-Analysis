Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

Information stored

Registration stores your name, username, email address, password hash, verification state, and profile changes. Passwords are hashed before storage.

Camera data

Connected camera addresses and captured alert frames may be stored by the installation. Configure retention and access controls for your environment.

Notifications and providers

Email addresses are used to deliver verification and alert messages through the SMTP provider configured by the operator.

Security

Protect environment files, database credentials, camera passwords, and secret keys. Review and harden this starter before production deployment.

Questions

For questions, visit Support.
